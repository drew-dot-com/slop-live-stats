What a sponsored pool is

On Slop Cash a project funds a monthly pool, contributors send pull requests, and the pool is split each month among the people whose work the maintainers accepted. Work that is not merged counts for nothing.

A sponsored pool is one where the Slop Cash treasury puts up the money instead of the project. SLOP holders choose which repos are sponsored in a decision market on Umia. Nothing is funded unless that market passes.

Who can enter

  • A public repo with an open-source licence.
  • It already merges pull requests from outside contributors.
  • It has no ban on AI-assisted contributions.
  • It is entered by one of its maintainers, with admin rights on the repo.

Repos run by Slop Cash's founders, or by a company a founder is a partner in, are left out of the first round.

How to enter

  1. List your repo at slop.cash/projects/new. Listing is free. When the setup asks you to install Slopbot, choose "Only select repositories" and pick this repo.
  2. Email hello@slop.cash with the link to your project's page on slop.cash, and say whether we may name your repo publicly.

Anyone can suggest a repo, but only its maintainers can enter it. A suggestion is a reason for us to write to them.

What maintainers get

  • A funded monthly pool that pays outside contributors for merged work on your repo, with no money from you.
  • A maintainer share, if you set one. A project can direct up to 30% of its monthly pool to the people who merge or close outside pull requests. You set it on your project's manage page, under Monthly rewards, and it starts with the next month.
  • Free Slopbot review of new pull requests, and a setting that caps how many outside pull requests the project takes at once.
  • A place on the slop.cash home page and in the project list that contributors' agents read, for as long as the pool is funded.

You are never required to merge anything. You decide what is accepted, as you do today.

What happens after you enter

  • We check each entry against the list above.
  • A repo is named in our posts, in any poll and in the proposal only after its maintainer has said yes.
  • The finalists go into one decision market, beside an outcome that funds nothing. The proposal states the repos, the amount for each and how many months it covers.
  • Holders trade the outcomes. A funding outcome has to finish above the do-nothing outcome by the market's threshold, which was 2% in earlier Slop Cash markets.

If your repo is funded

  • You create the project's vault on your manage page, which takes a couple of minutes. You own the vault.
  • Slop Cash deposits into it one month at a time. The treasury holds USDG on Robinhood Chain, so pools are expected to be funded in USDG there.
  • A month counts as funded when the vault holds its amount at the start of that UTC month, on top of anything still to be paid for the month before.
  • After each month ends, the pool is split among contributors with accepted work, and your maintainer share if you set one. Slop Cash deducts a 2% fee from each payout.
  • Sponsorship runs for the number of months in the proposal and then ends. Continuing it would need a new holder decision or your own funding.
  • Withdrawing sponsored funds from the vault ends the sponsorship.

What to know before entering

  • Entering is not a promise of funding. Holders may choose to fund nothing.
  • A funded pool attracts pull requests, many of them written with AI agents. The intake cap and Slopbot review are there for that, and the maintainer share pays for the time it takes.
  • Payouts run from the project's vault contract. Slop Cash holds the upgrade keys for these contracts and the key that confirms contributors' payout wallets.
  • Contributors need a wallet registered on slop.cash for the chain the pool is on. An amount set aside for someone without one waits for them.
